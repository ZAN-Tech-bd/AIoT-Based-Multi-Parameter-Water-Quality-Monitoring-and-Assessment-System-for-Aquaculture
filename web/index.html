Current sensor voltage—V
Calculated turbidity—NTU
  1. Put the sensor in clean, clear water, wait for Stable, press Capture clear-water voltage (0 NTU).
  2. Put it in a sample of known turbidity, enter its NTU value, wait for Stable, press Capture reference voltage.
  3. Check the preview and press Save Calibration.

Assessment target ranges

Not connected

These are generic starting values, not recommendations for your species or system. Set the range you actually want to hold. Values outside the range but within the warning margin show Warning; further out shows Outside Configured Range. Saved in the ESP32 flash so every phone and laptop sees the same ranges.

About this system

Where data lives

  • Sensor history: this browser tab only (RAM). Not on the ESP32, not in the cloud.
  • Calibration & settings: ESP32 flash (NVS), kept across restarts and power loss.
  • Works fully offline: no CDN, no cloud, no account.
